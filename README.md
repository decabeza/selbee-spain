# SELBEE SPAIN 🇪🇸

**Quantitative Research into Mathematical Edges in Spanish Lottery Games**

SELBEE SPAIN is an open-source quantitative research project exploring
mathematical inefficiencies and potential positive expected value (EV)
opportunities across Spanish lottery and betting games.

## Research objectives

- Analyse official lottery rules and prize structures.
- Collect and process historical results and prize distributions.
- Calculate probabilities and expected value.
- Investigate shared-prize dynamics.
- Run Monte Carlo simulations.
- Backtest statistical strategies.
- Identify and validate potential mathematical edges.

## Games under investigation

- Lototurf
- Quíntuple Plus
- La Quiniela
- Bonoloto
- La Primitiva
- Euromillones
- El Gordo de la Primitiva
- Lotería Nacional

## Technology stack

- Python 3.12+
- NumPy
- Pandas
- SciPy
- Matplotlib
- Streamlit
- Pytest

## Research principles

- Official data wherever available.
- Reproducible calculations.
- Out-of-sample validation.
- No assumptions of predictability in random draws.
- No strategy considered profitable without mathematical evidence.

## Disclaimer

This project is an independent statistical research experiment.
It does not guarantee profits or predict lottery outcomes.
